# Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)

**Definition:** A structured approach to ensure that an organization’s IT and business operations are aligned with strategic goals, effectively manage risk, and comply with applicable laws, regulations, and standards.

> **Objective:** Define clear IT and operational guidelines to support consistent, ethical, and risk-aware decision-making.

---

## Governance

- Establish policies and procedures to guide operations and decision-making (strategic decision framework)
- Define organizational objectives and measurable goals
- Assign roles and responsibilities for accountability
- Ensure actions align with organizational mission, values, and ethics
- Promote transparency, accountability, and ethical conduct

**Outcome:** Strategic oversight, clear decision-making structures, and alignment of IT and business objectives.

---

## Risk Management

- Identify, assess, and address threats to the organization
- Minimize the impact of unexpected events (e.g., cyberattacks, operational failures)
- Protect organizational resources, including data, systems, and personnel
- Support business continuity through recovery plans, backups, and incident response
- Enable informed decision-making and strategic opportunity management    

**Outcome:** Reduced operational and cybersecurity risks and enhanced resilience.

---

## Compliance

- Ensure adherence to legal, regulatory, contractual, and internal requirements
- Maintain certifications, licenses, and proof of compliance
- Protect organizational reputation, credibility, and stakeholder trust

**How:**

- Regular audits and assessments
- Continuous monitoring and reporting
- Training and awareness programs for employees

**Outcome:** Legal compliance, certified practices, and demonstrated accountability.
